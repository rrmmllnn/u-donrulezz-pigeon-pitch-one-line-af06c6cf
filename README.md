A city pigeon pitches investors on its startup: 40,000 New York pigeons as
same-day couriers, paid in breadcrumbs — with a statue in Central Park as CEO.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the one-line pitch (130 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

> Give us $500K and 40,000 New York pigeons become same-day couriers — paid in breadcrumbs, and our CEO is a statue in Central Park.
